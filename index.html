The house

Casa Maré sits on the same limestone shelf it's named for — maré, tide, because the caves beneath the terrace flood and empty twice a day, and we planned the house around watching it happen.

Nine rooms, one long terrace, a kitchen that cooks whatever the boats brought in that morning. No spa, no shuttle bus, no more than nine rooms — we found that's exactly how many the cliff can hold without crowding the view.
